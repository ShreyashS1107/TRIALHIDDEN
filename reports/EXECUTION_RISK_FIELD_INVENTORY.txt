EXECUTION / IMPLEMENTATION RISK FIELD INVENTORY & SIGNAL AUDIT
SIH 2026 Problem Statement SIH26103: MoSPI / IPMD Integrated Project-Monitoring Platform
==========================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------
This audit systematically reviews all candidate signals, features, and raw fields across the
PAIMANA master dataset (21,555 records, Apr 2025 - Jun 2026), the frozen feature dataset
(15,769 records, 98 columns), auxiliary datasets, and published monthly Flash Reports.

Key Audit Findings:
1. Physical Progress Signals: 11 distinct point-in-time features exist, capturing instantaneous
   progress, rolling velocity (1m/3m/6m), multi-month stagnation, and stagnation duration.
2. Schedule Behaviour Signals: 7 point-in-time features capture schedule slippage magnitude,
   overdue status, deadline proximity, and revision volatility.
3. Expenditure & Divergence Signals: 6 features quantify financial burn, monthly delta, and
   critically, the divergence spread between expenditure ratio and physical progress.
4. Lifecycle & Governance Context: 7 features capture project age, planned duration, size tier,
   state, and agency portfolio workload / mean execution rate.
5. Data & Reporting Quality: 5 features capture observation gaps, reporting consistency, and
   missing data indicators.
6. Structured Milestones & Qualitative Bottlenecks: A rigorous PDF text and schema audit confirms
   that PAIMANA Flash Reports DO NOT contain structured milestone tables, contractor performance
   scorecards, contractual dispute fields, or project-level bottleneck remark columns.

SECTION 1 — EXISTING SPECIFICATION ISSUES FOUND & RESOLVED
========================================================================================================================

Prior to this reconciliation, the following discrepancies and omissions existed in the backend specifications:

1. Absence of Pillar 2 (Operational Execution Surveillance):
   - The original backend specifications documented only predictive machine learning risk scores and basic Layer B
     engineering metrics.
   - The finalized Operational Execution-Stress Index (ESI), its 5 constituent stress components ($S_{\text{stag}},
     S_{\text{vel}}, S_{\text{sched}}, S_{\text{div}}, S_{\text{rep}}$), discrete warning flags, and alert hierarchy were missing.

2. Feature Universe Discrepancy (79 vs. 75 Features):
   - The specifications previously cited "79 point-in-time features" from an earlier exploratory inventory.
   - The certified modeling universe contains exactly 75 features (71 numerical + 4 categorical) because four raw date strings
     (`approval_start_date`, `original_completion_date`, `revised_doc_t`, `first_observed_month`) were excluded from the
     modeling matrix as their numerical transforms were already engineered.

3. OCMS Historical Data Role Ambiguity:
   - The specifications lacked an explicit boundary preventing OCMS historical priors from being ingested into the ML pipeline.
   - The empirical finding (`OCMS_EXPERIMENT_VALIDITY = VALID_AFTER_DOCUMENTATION_CORRECTION`) proved that adding OCMS priors
     degraded Out-of-Time predictive PR-AUC. The backend spec needed explicit separation of OCMS data into an analytics/context table.

4. Ambiguity Between Probabilities and Operational Indices:
   - `selected_integrated_risk` and `execution_stress_index` required explicit qualification as composite operational indices,
     distinguishing them from the calibrated statistical probability produced by the primary schedule delay model (`schedule_delay_risk`).

5. Lack of Standardized Prescriptive Monitoring Actions:
   - Automated mapping from observable execution stressors to formal MoSPI/IPMD administrative directives (`SITE_OBSTACLE_AUDIT`,
     `FINANCIAL_PHYSICAL_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT`, `RESOURCE_MOBILIZATION_DIRECTIVE`, `CRITICAL_PATH_RECALIBRATION`, `DATA_COMPLIANCE_DIRECTIVE`,
     `INTER_MINISTERIAL_COMMITTEE_ESCALATION`) was not codified in the API or database schemas.

6. Missing Execution-Specific Versioning Metadata:
   - Tables and API responses lacked `execution_index_version` and `feature_version` tracking alongside `model_version`.

SECTION 8 — THRESHOLD CLASSIFICATION & STANDARDIZATION

1. Candidate B Integrated Predictive Risk Thresholds (Historical Frozen ML Matrix):
   - Defined and validated in `ml/risk_engine/build_integrated_risk_score.py` and `integrated_risk_scores.csv`:
     * `LOW`       : $< 0.30$       (Empirical delay failure rate: 0.08%)

     * `HIGH`      : $0.60\text{–}<0.80$ (Empirical delay failure rate: 90.58%)
     * `VERY_HIGH` : $\ge 0.80$       (Empirical delay failure rate: 97.73%)

2. Operational Execution-Stress Alert Tiers (Finalized ESI Architecture):
   - Defined and finalized in `EXECUTION_RISK_FINAL_DECISION.txt` and `03_execution_stress_index.py`:
     * `NOMINAL`       : $< 0.35$          (~65.2% portfolio share)
     * `WATCH`         : $0.35\text{–}<0.55$ (~26.9% portfolio share)
     * `ATTENTION`     : $0.55\text{–}<0.75$ (~7.6% portfolio share)
     * `HIGH_PRIORITY` : $\ge 0.75$         (~0.2% portfolio share)

3. Standardized Cross-Quadrant Operational Monitoring Grid:
   - When cross-tabulating Integrated Predictive Risk against Execution Stress, the $0.55$ / $0.60$ threshold boundary cleanly
     separates standard surveillance from critical multi-ministerial escalation.

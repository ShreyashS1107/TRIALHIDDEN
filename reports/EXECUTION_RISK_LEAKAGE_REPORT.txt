2. AUDIT METHODOLOGY & VERIFICATION CHECKS
--------------------------------------------------
Every feature and signal evaluated at prediction month t was verified against 5 strict criteria:

  1. Temporal Ceiling Rule: Source evidence date must satisfy: evidence_date <= month_t.
     Verified: No feature references data from month t+1, t+2, or later.

  2. Revised Date Anti-Leakage Rule:
     If a project receives a schedule revision in month t+2, that revision date is NOT visible
     at month t. The feature 'schedule_slippage_months_t' uses strictly the revised date published
     in the official monthly flash report at or before month t.

  3. Progress Velocity Window Bounding:
     Rolling velocities (1m, 3m) are computed strictly backward using historical lags:
     velocity_3m_t = (progress_t - progress_t-3) / 3. Forward lookahead is strictly impossible.

  4. Expenditure Divergence Synchronicity:
     'divergence_spread_t' compares cumulative expenditure at month t against physical progress
     at month t. Both inputs originate synchronously from the exact same monthly reporting cycle.

  5. Cross-Sectional Independence:
     Agency-level and state-level context statistics are computed cross-sectionally within each
     individual month t, completely isolated from future monthly cohorts.

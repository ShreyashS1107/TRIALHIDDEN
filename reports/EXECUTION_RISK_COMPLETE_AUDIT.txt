SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & AUDIT MANDATE
==========================================================================================

Problem Statement SIH26103 explicitly requires an AI/ML-driven integrated project monitoring
platform capable of predictive identification of implementation/execution risks, early warning
of bottlenecks, and prescriptive monitoring recommendations.

As senior ML researchers, our mandate was to evaluate whether the existing empirical data
(PAIMANA monthly reports, April 2025 - June 2026) supports a supervised ML target for execution risk,
or whether an operational analytical index is the scientifically defensible construct.

CORE AUDIT FINDING:
  1. No valid supervised ML target exists due to severe reporting regime shifts (2.5x base rate collapse)
     and absence of ground-truth qualitative bottleneck labels.
  2. A multi-dimensional OPERATIONAL EXECUTION-STRESS INDEX (ESI) is fully supported, statistically
     robust, 100% point-in-time compliant, and provides crucial complementary monitoring value that
     predictive outcome models cannot capture.

SECTION 4: OPERATIONAL EXECUTION-STRESS INDEX (ESI) DESIGN & METHODOLOGY (PHASE 3)
==========================================================================================

To provide robust operational surveillance without uncalibrated probabilistic claims, we formulate
the OPERATIONAL EXECUTION-STRESS INDEX (ESI) as a deterministic point-in-time composite score [0.0 - 1.0]:

  ESI = 0.30 * S_stag + 0.25 * S_vel + 0.20 * S_div + 0.15 * S_sched + 0.10 * S_rep

Component Formulations:
  1. S_stag (30% weight): Stagnation Stress = clip(0.7 * (months_stag / 4) + 0.3 * (months_stag / 4 * rem_prog), 0, 1)
  2. S_vel  (25% weight): Velocity Stress = 1.0 if vel <= 0%, piecewise linear to 0.0 at vel >= 3.0%/month
  3. S_div  (20% weight): Divergence Stress = clip((expenditure_ratio% - physical_progress%) / 75.0, 0, 1)
  4. S_sched(15% weight): Schedule Debt Stress = 0.6 * clip(slippage / 36, 0, 1) + 0.4 * clip(overdue_mo / 24, 0, 1)
  5. S_rep  (10% weight): Quality Stress = 0.4 * obs_gap + 0.3 * miss_progress + 0.3 * clip(revisions / 3, 0, 1)

B. Prescriptive Monitoring Action Logic Matrix:

TRIGGER SIGNAL      : S_stag (Progress Stagnation Score >= 0.75 OR Consecutive Stagnant Months >= 3)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Zero physical progress recorded for 3+ consecutive reporting cycles while significant work remains.
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : SITE_OBSTACLE_AUDIT: Issue automated query to Implementing Agency regarding physical site access, land acquisition encumbrances, forest/environmental clearances, or contractor abandonment.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIGGER SIGNAL      : S_div (Expenditure / Progress Divergence > +25% spread)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Cumulative expenditure ratio significantly exceeds verified physical completion (e.g. 70% funds disbursed with < 35% physical build).
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : FINANCIAL_PHYSICAL_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT: Direct field verification of contractor billing against physical milestone completion; audit unspent mobilization advances and escrow balances.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIGGER SIGNAL      : S_vel (Progress Velocity < 0.5% / month with > 40% work pending)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Execution momentum has severely decelerated below minimum planned run-rate.
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : RESOURCE_MOBILIZATION_DIRECTIVE: Request joint review of contractor plant, machinery, and skilled labor deployment on critical path packages.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIGGER SIGNAL      : S_sched (Schedule Slippage >= 12 months OR Project Overdue > 6 months)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Project has breached original completion charter or received multiple formal extension revisions.
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : CRITICAL_PATH_RECALIBRATION: Convene Project Review Committee (PRC) to re-baseline critical path milestones and enforce revised commissioning covenants.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIGGER SIGNAL      : S_rep (Observation Gap Flag = 1 OR Missing Physical Progress = 1)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Project missed reporting cycles or failed to report physical progress on the PAIMANA portal.
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : DATA_COMPLIANCE_DIRECTIVE: Issue administrative compliance notice to Departmental Nodal Officer to restore mandatory monthly data submission.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIGGER SIGNAL      : COMPOUND (ESI >= 0.75 / High Priority Tier with >= 3 Concurrent Flags)
OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR: Concurrent physical stagnation, heavy expenditure divergence, and chronic schedule slippage.
PRESCRIPTIVE ACTION : INTER_MINISTERIAL_COMMITTEE_ESCALATION: Escalate to IPMD / MoSPI Central Monitoring Committee and Cabinet Secretariat Pragati framework for high-level dispute resolution.

SECTION 8: CONVENTIONAL STATISTICAL BASELINES & LEAKAGE AUDIT (PHASES 8, 9)
==========================================================================================

A. Conventional Statistical Baseline Comparison:
  - Single-variable heuristic rules (e.g. pure progress stagnation >= 3m) generate high false alarm rates
    because they fail to account for pending progress scale or financial alignment.
  - The multi-dimensional ESI outperforms single heuristics by synthesizing physical momentum, financial burn,
    and temporal debt into a single, calibrated operational score.

B. Point-in-Time Temporal Leakage Audit:
  - 13 out of 13 signals audited passed strict point-in-time constraints (100% compliant).
  - Zero future lookahead, zero forward-fill across t, and zero target contamination detected.
  - Full details in reports/EXECUTION_RISK_LEAKAGE_AUDIT.csv and reports/EXECUTION_RISK_LEAKAGE_REPORT.txt.

SECTION 9: LIMITATIONS & DATA CAPTURE ROADMAP
==========================================================================================

Current Platform Limitations:
  1. No qualitative root-cause logs in monthly tabular data (e.g. specific contractor disputes or court stays).
  2. Progress reporting quantization (self-reported percentages by line agencies).

Recommended MoSPI Data Capture Enhancements:
  * Standardized intermediate physical milestone schedules.
  * Standardized departmental bottleneck category dropdowns on the PAIMANA portal.
  * Concessionaire/contractor master linkage and performance tracking.

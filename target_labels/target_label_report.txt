2. EXACT TARGET DEFINITIONS
---------------------------
PRIMARY TARGET: schedule_delay_3m
  Meaning: At prediction month t, will the project breach its original completion date
           within or by the 3-month future evaluation horizon (t+3)?
  Baseline: original_completion_date (sanctioned baseline schedule).
  Positive Class (1): Project is actively ongoing at t+3 and t+3 > original_DOC,
                      OR official revised_DOC at t+3 > original_DOC,
                      OR completed in Table 3 with actual_completion_date > original_DOC.
  Negative Class (0): Project is active at t+3 with t+3 <= original_DOC and no revised delay,
                      OR completed in Table 3 on or before original_DOC.
  Unlabelled (NaN): Missing original DOC, or project exited before t+3 without Table 3 entry.

SECONDARY TARGET: cost_overrun_3m
  Meaning: At prediction month t, will the project's reported revised cost exceed its
           original sanctioned cost within or by the 3-month future evaluation horizon (t+3)?
  Baseline: original_cost_crore (sanctioned baseline budget).
  Positive Class (1): revised_cost at t+3 > original_cost,
                      OR completed in Table 3 with final cost > original_cost.
  Negative Class (0): revised_cost at t+3 <= original_cost,
                      OR completed in Table 3 with final cost <= original_cost.
  Unlabelled (NaN): Missing original/future cost, or unresolved project exit.

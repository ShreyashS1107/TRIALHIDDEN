--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. MENTOR HYPOTHESIS & MOTIVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The initial OCMS enrichment experiment tested injecting broad agency-level and
sector-level historical priors directly into the predictive ML models. That earlier
approach did not yield incremental predictive value because broad sector averages
obscured individual project execution dynamics.

The mentor suggested an alternative, project-similarity approach:
Instead of broad historical aggregates, identify projects that have longitudinal
continuity between the legacy OCMS (2011–2025) and active PAIMANA (2025–2026) eras.
By clustering projects on their pre-transition physical and financial characteristics,
the system could establish distinct project archetypes and derive point-in-time
historical benchmarks to test for incremental predictive value.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. PHASE 1: COHORT SELECTION & PROJECT-ARCHETYPE CLUSTERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cohort Selection: From 2,741 total PAIMANA projects, exactly 1,442 unique projects
  with comprehensive longitudinal OCMS history (Cohort A) were identified.
- Representation: Projects were represented using 6 normalized pre-transition
  characteristics (original cost, planned duration, pre-PAIMANA slippage, cost revisions,
  schedule revisions, and expenditure velocity).
- Stability & Validation: Standardized K-Means established an optimal, highly stable
  K = 4 archetype partitioning:
  * Silhouette Score: 0.4174 (vs K=3: 0.3541, K=5: 0.3812)
  * Calinski-Harabasz Index: 570.64 | Davies-Bouldin Index: 1.2146
  * Seed Stability (ARI): 0.9992 ± 0.0022
  * 100-Iteration Bootstrap ARI: 0.9779 ± 0.0105
- The 4 Discovered Physical Project Archetypes:
  1. Cluster 0: High Administrative Cost-Revision Volatility (N=252, 17.5%)
  2. Cluster 1: Standard Rapid-Execution / Linear Infrastructure (N=946, 65.6%)
  3. Cluster 2: Severe Legacy Stagnation & Chronic Slippage (N=69, 4.8%)
  4. Cluster 3: Mega Capital High-Value Infrastructure (N=175, 12.1%)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. PHASE 2A: POINT-IN-TIME HISTORICAL ARCHETYPE BENCHMARKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Snapshot Coverage: 15,769 prediction snapshots across 12 monthly prediction epochs
  (Apr 2025 through Mar 2026).
- Strict Point-in-Time Constraint: For every prediction at month T, all archetype
  statistics were derived strictly from historical evidence available prior to month T
  (T_evidence < T_prediction).
- Temporal Causal Leakage Audit: ZERO violations (15,769 / 15,769 rows certified).
- Archetype Outcome Differences Established:
  * Cluster 0: 10.5% historical completion delay rate, 99.2% pre-transition cost revisions.
  * Cluster 1: 77.2% delay rate, 26.0 months median delay (tracking national baseline).
  * Cluster 2: 251.0 months (~20.9 years) median historical delay, 100.0% revision rate.
  * Cluster 3: ₹7,588 Cr mean budget, 95.6 months planned duration, 0.0m early slippage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. PHASE 2B: CONTROLLED PREDICTIVE ML EXPERIMENT RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A strictly controlled predictive experiment was executed comparing Model A (frozen
75-feature PAIMANA-only baseline) against Model B (PAIMANA + 6 mathematically
audited, point-in-time safe archetype benchmark features).

Evaluated across the frozen Out-of-Time (OOT) test period (Feb–Mar 2026, 3,871 snapshots):

Target                   Model A (PAIMANA)    Model B (+ Benchmarks)    Delta (B - A)    Empirical Finding
-----------------------  -------------------  ------------------------  ---------------  -------------------------------------------
Schedule Delay (3m)      0.9722 PR-AUC        0.9713 PR-AUC             -0.0009          No lift; PAIMANA baseline strictly superior
Cost Overrun State (3m)  0.9881 PR-AUC        0.9881 PR-AUC             ~0.0000          Exact tie; 0 incremental discrimination
Schedule Revision (3m)   0.0519 PR-AUC        0.0641 PR-AUC             +0.0121          Modest PR lift, but calibration worsened

Calibration Analysis on OOT Test Split:
- Schedule Delay: Model A Brier = 0.0391 | Model B Brier = 0.0394 (Model B slightly worse)
- Schedule Revision: Model A Brier = 0.0176 | Model B Brier = 0.0212 (Model B significantly worse; ECE worsened from 0.0202 to 0.0236)

Feature Ablation & Redundancy Insights:
- Schedule Delay & Cost Overrun are dominated by real-time PAIMANA features
  (`months_to_original_doc_t`, `cost_escalation_pct_t`, `current_schedule_status`).
- Broad historical archetype outcome rates added zero predictive signal (Delta <= 0).
- The minor PR-AUC lift in schedule revision was driven entirely by the static legacy
  linkage flag (`is_legacy_linked`), not archetype benchmarking, and resulted in
  overconfident probability calibration on non-revising legacy projects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. FINAL EXPERIMENTAL DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BENCHMARK_LAYER_DECISION = NO_CLEAR_INCREMENTAL_VALUE

Under the controlled out-of-time experiment, archetype-based OCMS historical
benchmark features did not provide clear incremental predictive value beyond the
frozen PAIMANA-only feature set.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. PRODUCTION ML DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRODUCTION_ML_CHANGE = NO

Do NOT add benchmark features to the frozen 75-feature production ML model.
Retain the champion PAIMANA-only production models (RF_02 Calibrated, Balanced RF,
Logistic Regression L2).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. CONTEXTUAL BENCHMARKING & DECISION-SUPPORT DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retain the OCMS longitudinal layer:
  OCMS Longitudinal Data -> Project Archetypes (K=4) -> Historical Peer Benchmarks

as a separate historical intelligence, explainability, and contextual decision-support
layer within the MoSPI executive dashboard. This provides human project officers
with valuable peer reference baselines without contaminating the automated ML
predictive pipeline.

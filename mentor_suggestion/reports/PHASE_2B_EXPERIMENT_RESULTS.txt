1. EXECUTIVE EXPERIMENT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This experiment evaluated whether adding historical project-archetype benchmarks
(derived from longitudinal OCMS–PAIMANA linked projects) provides incremental
predictive value beyond the frozen 75-feature PAIMANA-only production baseline.

Experimental Design Safeguards:
- Production Feature Universe: Exactly 75 features (71 numerical + 4 categorical).
  Zero modifications to production code, weights, or feature store definitions.
- Preprocessing: Numerical median imputation + StandardScaler, categorical OneHotEncoder.
- Temporal Partitioning: Strict forward chronological split (Train -> Val -> OOT).
- Benchmark Feature Set: 6 mathematically audited, point-in-time safe features.
- Primary Decision Criterion: Out-of-Time (OOT) generalization on Feb–Mar 2026.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STATISTICAL & PRACTICAL SIGNIFICANCE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Schedule Delay: Adding archetype benchmarks produces a slight degradation in
   OOT PR-AUC (-0.0009) and ROC-AUC (-0.0013) while worsening Brier score (+0.0003).
   The PAIMANA-only baseline is strictly superior.
2. Cost Overrun: Model A and Model B are completely tied on OOT PR-AUC (0.9881 vs
   0.9881) and ROC-AUC (0.9895 vs 0.9894). Benchmark features add zero incremental
   predictive discrimination.
3. Schedule Revision: While Model B produces a small statistical PR-AUC increase
   (+0.0121, 0.0519 -> 0.0641), this gain comes with degraded calibration (Brier:
   0.0176 -> 0.0212) and yields an operational F1 of only 0.1176. The signal is
   entirely driven by legacy project status rather than archetype similarity.

4. HISTORICAL OUTCOME DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Historical Schedule Delay:
   - Evaluated as: $\text{historical\_delay\_months} = (y_{\text{act}} - y_{\text{orig}}) \times 12 + (m_{\text{act}} - m_{\text{orig}})$.
   - Delay Flag: $\text{historical\_delay\_flag} = 1.0$ if $\text{delay\_months} > 0$, else $0.0$.
B. Historical Revision Frequencies:
   - Shift in official commissioning date ($DOC_k \ne DOC_{k-1}$) in pre-transition snapshot history.
   - Shift in sanctioned cost ($|Cost_k - Cost_{k-1}| > 0.05$ Cr) in pre-transition snapshot history.
C. Historical Stagnation Rate:
   - Frequency of extended pre-transition physical progress stalls ($\ge 3$ consecutive months of zero progress).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. POINT-IN-TIME METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each prediction epoch $t \in [\text{2025-04}, \text{2026-03}]$:
- Completed Project Evidence Filter: $\text{actual\_completion\_date} < t$.
- Causal Isolation: Zero information from $t_{\text{completion}} \ge t$ is included in the benchmark statistics for month $t$.
- Frozen Centroid Projection: Unmatched projects ($N = 1,299$) are mapped to the nearest frozen archetype centroid using
  baseline parameters, maintaining $100\%$ universe coverage across all 15,769 snapshots.

10. SMOOTHING METHOD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent rate instability for clusters with moderate sample sizes, an Empirical Bayes Beta shrinkage estimator was applied:
$$\text{DelayRate}_{\text{smoothed}}(k, t) = \frac{n_{\text{delayed}}(k, <t) + M \cdot P_{\text{portfolio}}(<t)}{N_{\text{completed}}(k, <t) + M}$$
- Smoothing Strength: $M = 5.0$ pseudo-observations.
- Portfolio Prior: $P_{\text{portfolio}}(<t) = 82.66\%$.

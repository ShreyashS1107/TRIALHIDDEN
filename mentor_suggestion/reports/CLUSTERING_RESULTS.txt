1. EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of Phase 1 is to build and validate a rigorous, point-in-time safe project-level clustering pipeline using
the population of capital infrastructure projects that exhibit verified longitudinal overlap between the legacy Online
Computerised Monitoring System (OCMS, 2011–2025) and the active Project Administration and Integrated Monitoring
Application (PAIMANA, 2025–2026).

This phase establishes whether capital infrastructure projects can be meaningfully segmented into distinct, stable, and
interpretable structural project archetypes using baseline project characteristics and pre-transition historical dynamics,
laying the foundation for cluster-level historical benchmarking without altering production models.

4. COHORT DEFINITION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The authoritative clustering cohort (Cohort A) is defined as:
$$\text{Cohort A} = \{ p \in \text{PAIMANA} \mid \text{legacy\_ocms\_code}_p \ne \emptyset \land \text{pre\_paimana\_observation\_months}_p \ge 1 \}$$

  pre-transition monthly snapshot observation.
- Point-in-Time Boundary: Evaluated strictly as-of April 2025 ($t \le \text{2025-03}$).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. VARIABLES SELECTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following 6 continuous dimensions were selected for the primary numerical clustering matrix:
1. `original_cost_log`: Normalizes right-skewed budget magnitudes spanning ₹150 Cr to ₹50,000+ Cr.
2. `planned_duration_months`: Captures structural engineering complexity and project scale.
3. `pre_paimana_obs_months`: Represents historical vintage and pre-transition monitoring exposure.
4. `historical_schedule_revision_count`: Reflects pre-transition administrative schedule volatility.
5. `historical_cost_revision_count`: Reflects pre-transition scope amendments and financial volatility.
6. `pre_paimana_max_schedule_slippage_months`: Captures inherited legacy execution momentum and distress.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. VARIABLES REJECTED & JUSTIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. `project_id`, `legacy_ocms_code`, `project_name`: REJECTED. Project identifiers must never be clustering features.
2. `clean_state`: REJECTED from Euclidean distance space. State is high-cardinality categorical; including it creates geographic
   islands rather than physical execution archetypes. (Retained as metadata diagnostic).
3. `agency`: REJECTED from distance space. 1,065 high-cardinality agency strings introduce extreme sparsity and fragmented earlier
   prior experiments. (Retained for post-hoc cluster profiling).
4. `pre_paimana_max_cost_escalation_pct`: REJECTED from primary distance vector due to extreme heavy-tailed outliers
   ($> 10^7\%$ in legacy OCR edge cases) that distort Euclidean distance scaling even after standard scaling.
5. `cumulative_expenditure_crore`: REJECTED. Historical spend without verified completion status conflates cash disbursement
   with physical progress.
6. Post-April 2025 variables & targets (`schedule_delay_3m`, `cost_overrun_state_3m`, etc.): REJECTED. Strict prohibition of
   future information.

13. SELECTED K & JUSTIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SELECTED K = 4

Empirical Justification:
1. Multi-Archetype Discriminative Power: While $K=2$ achieves higher raw silhouette (0.5379), it creates a trivial split
   (small vs mega), failing to isolate chronic slippage from administrative volatility.
2. Silhouette & Separation Peak: Among multi-cluster configurations ($K \ge 3$), $K=4$ achieves the highest Silhouette
   Score (**0.4174**), high Calinski-Harabasz score (**570.64**), and low Davies-Bouldin index (**1.2146**).
3. Sample Size Viability: Every cluster in the $K=4$ solution contains sufficient projects ($N \ge 69$), well exceeding
   the minimum stability threshold ($N \ge 30$) required for robust historical benchmarking.
4. Absence of Micro-Clusters: $K \ge 6$ causes severe silhouette degradation ($< 0.28$) and creates isolated micro-clusters ($N \le 22$).

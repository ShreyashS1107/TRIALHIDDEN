1. TEMPORAL RULE VERIFICATION (T_evidence < T_prediction)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every benchmark statistic and point-in-time contextual feature utilized during
Phase 2B training, validation, and out-of-time testing was audited against the
strict forward-temporal constraint:

  For any prediction snapshot at prediction month T:
  Evidence Date < Prediction Month T

Audit Findings:
1. Historical Outcome Pool:
   - All completed project statistics (historical delay rates, median delay durations,
     and cost revision rates) were computed exclusively over projects completed
     prior to the PAIMANA prediction window.
   - Zero future completed outcomes from active PAIMANA monitoring were allowed
     into historical benchmark numerators or denominators.
2. Point-in-Time Trajectory Statistics:
   - All project-level trajectory metrics (physical progress, slippage, expenditure)
     were computed strictly using snapshots observed at or prior to epoch T.
   - Zero future monthly reports, zero future cost revisions, and zero future
     completion date revisions were accessible at prediction time.
3. Target Label Isolation:
   - 3-month forward target labels (`schedule_delay_3m`, `cost_overrun_state_3m`,
     `schedule_revision_3m`) were strictly isolated from the feature matrix.
   - Zero target label columns or target metadata were passed into the feature pipeline.
4. ESI & Risk Engine Independence:
   - Execution Stress Index (ESI) was completely excluded from all feature sets.
   - Production risk engine outputs and ML predictions were not used as inputs.

1. SCIENTIFIC & EMPIRICAL JUSTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------
Based on a rigorous 10-phase empirical audit of the PAIMANA dataset (21,555 master records,
15,769 frozen feature records, 15 reporting months from April 2025 to June 2026), the senior ML
research team concludes that:

A. SUPERVISED ML TARGET IS NOT SUPPORTED:
   - Candidate supervised targets (e.g. future progress stagnation delta <= 0% over t -> t+3)
     suffer from a severe 2.5x base rate collapse (37.3% in Train to 16.1% in Validation) caused by
     administrative reporting regime shifts (PAIMANA Table 1 to Table 6 transition) rather than
     real-world project acceleration.
   - Published PAIMANA reports contain NO official ground-truth qualitative bottleneck labels,
     milestone tables, contractor scorecards, or contractual dispute fields.
   - Empirical classifiers fitted on candidate stagnation targets exhibit poor generalization on
     Out-of-Time test data (Logistic Regression ROC-AUC = 0.5585, RF PR-AUC = 0.4739).
   - Manufacturing synthetic labels to train a 4th ML model would violate scientific integrity,
     introduce circular logic, and degrade platform credibility.

B. OPERATIONAL EXECUTION-STRESS INDEX (ESI) IS STRONGLY SUPPORTED:
   - 13 point-in-time compliant execution signals exist with 100% temporal audit compliance
     and zero future lookahead leakage.
   - The 5-dimensional Operational Execution-Stress Index (ESI) combines:
       1. S_stag (30%): Physical Progress Stagnation Stress
       2. S_vel  (25%): Progress Velocity Deterioration Stress
       3. S_div  (20%): Expenditure/Progress Divergence Stress
       4. S_sched(15%): Schedule Slippage Magnitude & Overdue Debt
       5. S_rep  (10%): Observation Quality & Revision Volatility
   - ESI exhibits low rank correlation with Integrated Predictive Risk (Spearman rho = 0.1360),
     proving it captures non-redundant, distinct operational information.
   - Crucially, 65.4% of high-stress execution alerts (809 project-months) occur on projects
     classified as Low or Moderate by predictive outcome models (e.g. early-stage stalled projects).
   - ESI powers a robust 4-tier Early Warning Framework (Nominal, Watch, Attention, High Priority)
     and a deterministic Prescriptive Monitoring Action Logic Matrix for MoSPI / IPMD.

--------------------------------------------------

  PILLAR 1: PREDICTIVE OUTCOME ENGINE (Frozen Production ML Models)

    - Prescriptive Logic      : Automated mapping from observed stress components to MoSPI administrative actions.
    - Purpose                 : Real-time site friction detection and bottleneck early warning.

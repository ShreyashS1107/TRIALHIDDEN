SECTION 2 — SUMMARY OF CORRECTIONS & ENHANCEMENTS MADE
========================================================================================================================

1. Integration of Pillar 2 (Operational Execution Surveillance):
   - Problem in Prior Spec: The frontend specification previously focused exclusively on Predictive ML Risk and Layer B
     derived metrics, completely omitting the Execution-Stress Index (ESI).
   - Correction: Added comprehensive specifications for the deterministic Operational Execution-Stress Index (ESI),
     its 5 constituent stress dimensions ($S_{\text{stag}}, S_{\text{vel}}, S_{\text{div}}, S_{\text{sched}}, S_{\text{rep}}$),
     5 discrete operational flags, alert tiers ('NOMINAL', 'WATCH', 'ATTENTION', 'HIGH_PRIORITY'), and dominant stressor.

2. Cross-Quadrant & Non-Redundancy Justification:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: Did not explain the critical operational relationship between predictive risk and execution stress.
   - Correction: Formulated the 2x2 dual-perspective surveillance grid and documented the empirical evidence ($\rho = 0.1360$,
     $65.4\%$ off-diagonal high-stress alerts occurring on Low/Moderate predictive risk projects).

3. Prescriptive Monitoring Action Logic Framework:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: Lacked standardized prescriptive monitoring recommendations for monitoring officers.
   - Correction: Codified the 6 approved administrative action directives (`SITE_OBSTACLE_AUDIT`, `FINANCIAL_PHYSICAL_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT`,
     `RESOURCE_MOBILIZATION_DIRECTIVE`, `CRITICAL_PATH_RECALIBRATION`, `DATA_COMPLIANCE_DIRECTIVE`, `INTER_MINISTERIAL_COMMITTEE_ESCALATION`).

4. Tri-Source Alerting Architecture:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: Alerting rules merged ML predictions and operational rules without clear categorical separation.
   - Correction: Formally separated alerts into **Predictive ML Alerts**, **Execution Surveillance Alerts**, and **Rule-Based Flags**.

5. OCMS Historical Data Context Policy:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: Did not clearly delineate OCMS historical data from real-time ML inputs.
   - Correction: Codified `OCMS_EXPERIMENT_VALIDITY = VALID_AFTER_DOCUMENTATION_CORRECTION`, strictly banning OCMS priors from ML
     inference features and restricting OCMS data to historical benchmarking cards and context curves.

6. Provenance & Versioning Metadata:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: Lacked explicit requirements for displaying snapshot epochs and model/pipeline versions.
   - Correction: Added `report_month`, `scored_at`, `model_version`, `feature_version`, and `execution_index_version`.

7. Unified Frontend-to-Backend JSON Contract & Field Traceability Matrix:
   - Problem in Prior Spec: JSON schema lacked Pillar 2 execution fields and full field traceability.
   - Correction: Updated Part 18 JSON contract and added Part 19 Master Field Traceability & Reconciliation Matrix.

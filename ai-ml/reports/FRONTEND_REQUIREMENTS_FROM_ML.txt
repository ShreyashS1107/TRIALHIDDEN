PART 1 — THE ACTIVE AI/ML TWO-PILLAR ARCHITECTURE
========================================================================================================================

The AI/ML subsystem is organized into TWO complementary, non-overlapping analytical pillars:

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 PILLAR 1: PREDICTIVE OUTCOME ENGINE (3-Month Fixed-Horizon ML Forecasting)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Forecasts the statistical probability and likelihood of adverse terminal delivery outcomes over the next 3 months:

1. Primary Model: Schedule Delay Risk (`schedule_delay_risk`)
   - Champion Model      : RF_02 (Tuned Random Forest: 200 trees, max_depth=12, min_samples_leaf=5, sqrt features).
   - Calibration Pipeline: Platt Sigmoid Scaling (fitted strictly on Validation set logits via LogisticRegression).
   - Out-of-Time Metrics : ROC-AUC = 0.9723, PR-AUC = 0.9722, Calibrated Brier Score = 0.0389, ECE = 0.0279.
   - Nature & Type       : CALIBRATED PROBABILITY in range [0.0000, 1.0000] (0.0% to 100.0%).
   - Operational Meaning : Model-estimated calibrated probability of breaching the sanctioned completion date within 3 months.

2. Secondary Model 1: Cost Overrun Risk (`cost_overrun_risk`)
   - Champion Model      : Balanced Random Forest (200 trees, max_depth=12, min_samples_leaf=5, class_weight='balanced').
   - Out-of-Time Metrics : ROC-AUC = 0.9895, PR-AUC = 0.9881, Brier Score = 0.0386, Precision@Top10% = 99.74%, Lift = 3.13x.
   - Nature & Type       : CONTINUOUS MODEL SCORE / PROBABILITY in range [0.0000, 1.0000].
   - Operational Meaning : Likelihood that the project will operate in a structural budget overrun state (> original budget).

3. Secondary Model 2: Schedule Revision Warning (`schedule_revision_risk`)
   - Champion Model      : Regularized Logistic Regression (L2 penalty, unweighted, C=1.0).
   - Out-of-Time Metrics : ROC-AUC = 0.8264, PR-AUC = 0.0519, Precision@Top10% = 4.22%, Lift = 3.55x.
   - Nature & Type       : MODEL PROBABILITY / WARNING SCORE in range [0.0000, 1.0000].
   - Operational Meaning : Early warning signal for upcoming administrative completion date extensions or milestone re-baselining.

4. Multi-Dimensional Integrated Predictive Risk Index (`selected_integrated_risk` / `integrated_risk`)
   - Candidate B Formula : Integrated_Risk = (0.50 * schedule_delay_risk) + (0.35 * cost_overrun_risk) + (0.15 * schedule_revision_risk)
   - Nature & Type       : OPERATIONAL TRIAGE INDEX in range [0.0000, 1.0000].
   - CRITICAL GUARDRAIL  : MUST NOT be labeled as a "calibrated probability of project failure". It is an operational prioritization index.

5. Predictive Risk Bands (`risk_band`):
   - Production Tiers    : LOW (< 0.30) | MODERATE (0.30 to < 0.60) | HIGH (0.60 to < 0.80) | VERY_HIGH (>= 0.80).
   - Standardized Tiers  : LOW (< 0.35) | MODERATE (0.35 to < 0.55) | HIGH (0.55 to < 0.75) | VERY_HIGH (>= 0.75).
   - Monotonicity        : Verified monotonic increasing failure rates on untouched OOT holdout (Delay rate: 0.08% -> 97.73%).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 PILLAR 2: OPERATIONAL EXECUTION SURVEILLANCE (Point-in-Time Site Friction)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Monitors current observable site execution friction, physical stagnation, and financial divergence at month t:

1. Scientific Finding: `SUPERVISED_EXECUTION_RISK_TARGET = NOT_SUPPORTED`
   - Published PAIMANA reports do not contain qualitative contractor dispute records, court injunction logs, or milestone tables.
   - Candidate stagnation targets suffer from a 2.5x base-rate collapse caused by reporting structure transitions.
   - Supervised execution models fail Out-of-Time generalization (Logistic Regression ROC-AUC = 0.5585).
   - Therefore, the execution surveillance layer is formulated as a deterministic, leak-free analytical index.

2. Operational Execution-Stress Index (ESI) Formula:
   ESI = (0.30 * S_stag) + (0.25 * S_vel) + (0.20 * S_div) + (0.15 * S_sched) + (0.10 * S_rep)
   - Nature & Type       : DETERMINISTIC ANALYTICAL INDEX in range [0.0000, 1.0000].
   - CRITICAL GUARDRAIL  : ESI is NOT an ML probability, NOT a prediction of project failure, and NOT a causal diagnosis.
                           It measures observable site friction and operational stress right now.

3. The 5 Constituent Stress Dimensions:
   - $S_{\text{stag}}$ (30% weight): Physical progress stagnation stress (months of zero progress + remaining work scale).
   - $S_{\text{vel}}$  (25% weight): Progress velocity deterioration stress (recent 3-month construction speed < 0.5%/mo).
   - $S_{\text{div}}$  (20% weight): Capital burn vs physical progress divergence stress (spend ratio exceeding progress > 25%).
   - $S_{\text{sched}}$(15% weight): Schedule slippage magnitude and overdue debt stress (accumulated delay months).
   - $S_{\text{rep}}$  (10% weight): Observation quality, reporting gaps, and revision volatility friction.

4. ESI Operational Alert Tiers (`esi_tier` / `execution_stress_band`):
   - 'NOMINAL'       : ESI < 0.35          (~65.2% of projects; Smooth physical progress, balanced spending)
   - 'WATCH'         : 0.35 <= ESI < 0.55  (~26.9% of projects; Early deceleration, 1-2 months stagnation)
   - 'ATTENTION'     : 0.55 <= ESI < 0.75  (~7.6% of projects; Confirmed stagnation >= 3m OR divergence > 25%)
   - 'HIGH_PRIORITY' : ESI >= 0.75         (~0.2% of projects; Critical deadlock across 3+ dimensions)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                          a 10x fiscal base-rate collapse (21.9% -> 0.9%).

PART 2 — DEFINITIVE USER-FACING ML OUTPUT SPECIFICATION (PILLAR 1)

The frontend must accurately display each of the following predictive ML fields:

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Field Name: schedule_delay_risk
   - Display Label      : "Schedule Delay Risk (3-Month)"
   - Simple Meaning     : Model-estimated probability that the project will experience a schedule delay over the next 3 months.
   - Expected Range     : [0.0000, 1.0000] (Display as percentage: 0.0% to 100.0%).
   - Output Nature      : Calibrated Probability (Sigmoid / Platt scaling on RF_02).
   - User Display       : Direct percentage gauge, progress indicator, or numeric badge.
   - Guardrail Language : "Estimated probability of project exceeding sanctioned completion date within 3 months."

2. Field Name: cost_overrun_risk
   - Display Label      : "Cost Overrun Risk (3-Month)"
   - Simple Meaning     : Model-estimated risk score indicating likelihood of project being in a structural budget overrun state.
   - Expected Range     : [0.0000, 1.0000] (Display as score: 0.00 to 1.00 or percentage).
   - Output Nature      : Continuous Model Score / Probability (Balanced Random Forest).
   - User Display       : Continuous risk score bar / gauge.
   - Guardrail Language : "Model score measuring statistical likelihood of project exceeding sanctioned baseline cost."

3. Field Name: schedule_revision_risk
   - Display Label      : "Schedule Revision Risk (3-Month)"
   - Simple Meaning     : Model-estimated early warning score indicating likelihood of upcoming administrative milestone extension.
   - Expected Range     : [0.0000, 1.0000] (Display as score: 0.00 to 1.00 or percentage).
   - Output Nature      : Model Probability / Warning Score (Logistic Regression).
   - User Display       : Secondary warning badge / gauge.
   - Guardrail Language : "Early warning indicator for potential administrative deadline revisions."

4. Field Name: selected_integrated_risk (or integrated_risk)
   - Display Label      : "Integrated Risk Index"
   - Simple Meaning     : Multi-dimensional composite surveillance index synthesizing schedule delay, financial expansion, and administrative rescheduling risks.
   - Expected Range     : [0.0000, 1.0000] (Index score from 0.00 to 1.00).
   - Output Nature      : Composite Operational Index: (0.50 * Schedule) + (0.35 * Cost) + (0.15 * Revision).
   - User Display       : Primary sorting metric, portfolio summary cards, and project risk headers.
   - Guardrail Language : "Composite surveillance index for portfolio prioritization. (Operational index, not a probability)."

5. Field Name: risk_band
   - Display Label      : "Predictive Risk Band" / "Risk Tier"
   - Simple Meaning     : Triage category for monitoring urgency based on the integrated predictive score.
   - Expected Values    : 'LOW', 'MODERATE', 'HIGH', 'VERY_HIGH'.
   - User Display       : Prominent status badge / tag across all views.
   - Explanatory Text   :
     * LOW       : Score < 0.30 (Low combined risk signals; progressing within baseline)
     * MODERATE  : 0.30 <= Score < 0.60 (Emerging risk signals; routine surveillance required)
     * HIGH      : 0.60 <= Score < 0.80 (Elevated combined risk signals; active management review required)
     * VERY_HIGH : Score >= 0.80 (Strongest combined distress signals; priority executive intervention)

6. Field Name: dominant_component
   - Display Label      : "Primary Risk Pillar" / "Dominant Factor"
   - Simple Meaning     : The specific predictive pillar contributing the largest weighted share to the integrated risk score.
   - Expected Values    : 'Schedule Delay', 'Cost Overrun', 'Schedule Revision'.
   - User Display       : Pill badge displayed alongside the integrated risk score.
   - Guardrail Language : "Indicates which risk dimension contributes most heavily to the overall predictive score."

7. Field Names: schedule_contribution, cost_contribution, schedule_revision_contribution
   - Display Labels     : "Schedule Component", "Cost Component", "Revision Component"
   - Simple Meaning     : Absolute weighted contribution of each pillar:
                          - schedule_contribution = 0.50 * schedule_delay_risk [0.0000, 0.5000]
                          - cost_contribution = 0.35 * cost_overrun_risk [0.0000, 0.3500]
                          - schedule_revision_contribution = 0.15 * schedule_revision_risk [0.0000, 0.1500]
   - User Display       : Suitable for stacked bar charts, waterfall breakdowns, or radar charts.

8. Field Names: schedule_contrib_pct, cost_contrib_pct, schedule_rev_contrib_pct
   - Display Labels     : "Schedule Share (%)", "Cost Share (%)", "Revision Share (%)"
   - Simple Meaning     : Normalized percentage share of total risk generated by each pillar (sums to 100.0%).
   - User Display       : Suitable for donut charts, attribution bars, or tooltip breakdowns.

12. `months_since_last_progress_increase`: Consecutive months at 0% physical progress increase
13. `expenditure_progress_divergence`   : Expenditure ratio (%) minus physical progress (%) spread

     * Dominant Risk Factor: Primary driver ('Schedule Delay', 'Cost Overrun', 'Schedule Revision').
   - PILLAR 2 (EXECUTION SURVEILLANCE):
     * Execution-Stress Index (ESI): Deterministic site friction score [0.00 to 1.00].
     * Execution Surveillance Tier: Status badge ('NOMINAL', 'WATCH', 'ATTENTION', 'HIGH_PRIORITY').
     * Dominant Stressor: Primary site issue ('Progress Velocity Collapse', 'Physical Progress Stagnation', etc.).
     * Active Stress Flags: Count and chips for active warning signals (0 to 5 flags).
     * Suggested Monitoring Action: Prescriptive directive for MoSPI monitoring officers.

3. UNDERSTAND WHY BOTH PILLARS MATTER:
   Predictive Risk evaluates future outcome likelihood. ESI evaluates immediate site friction today.
   A project can be Low Predictive Risk but High Execution Stress (early-stage project stalled at 0% progress).
   Highlight these off-diagonal projects on your dashboard!

4. DO NOT INVENT "REASONS FOR DELAY":
   The ML models do not know real-world physical causes (contractor bankruptcy, legal stays, or monsoons).
   Show the mathematical risk components and real engineering metrics (velocity, stagnation streaks, budget escalation).

5. OCMS HISTORICAL DATA IS CONTEXT ONLY:
   OCMS data provides multi-year agency and sector benchmarks. It is not an input to the real-time ML models.

6. PROVENANCE & SNAPSHOTS:
   Always display the reporting epoch (`report_month`, e.g. "2026-03") so officers know which snapshot they are viewing.

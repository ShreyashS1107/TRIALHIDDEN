1. OBJECTIVE & EXPERIMENTAL SCOPE
---------------------------------
Predict the 3-month forward operational schedule delay (schedule_delay_3m) for major infrastructure projects
monitored by MoSPI/IPMD. Formulated strictly as a point-in-time probabilistic classification problem:
P(schedule_delay_{t+3} = 1 | X_{<=t}). The model generates risk probabilities, risk strata, and top factor associations.

2. DATASET SCALE & TEMPORAL PARTITIONS
--------------------------------------
- Master Prediction Snapshots : 15,769 total candidate snapshots
- Train Partition (2025-04 to 2025-11): 8,814 total | 5,063 usable labelled (Positives: 2,301 / 45.45%)
- Validation Set (2025-12 to 2026-01): 3,084 total | 3,043 usable labelled (Positives: 1,961 / 64.44%)
- OOT Test Set   (2026-02 to 2026-03): 3,871 total | 3,787 usable labelled (Positives: 2,227 / 58.81%)

3. PREPROCESSING & ANTI-LEAKAGE DISCIPLINE
------------------------------------------
- Imputation and scaling transformers were fitted STRICTLY on the training set (2025-04 to 2025-11).
- Zero test/validation data leakage into encoding or scaling pipelines.
- Target columns, label metadata, project IDs, and raw future fields were strictly excluded from X.
- Total active feature dimensions in X: 75 features (71 numeric + 4 categorical).

4. MODELS EVALUATED
-------------------
1. Majority Baseline: Prior empirical probability predictor.
2. Rule-Based Persistence Baseline: High risk if schedule_slippage > 0 OR stagnant_3m == 1 OR (progress_velocity <= 0 and progress < 100).
3. Logistic Regression: L2-regularized linear model with median imputation + standard scaling.
4. Random Forest: 100 trees, max_depth=12, min_samples_leaf=5.
5. Gradient Boosted Trees: Histogram-based GBDT (HistGradientBoosting), max_depth=6, min_samples_leaf=20, native NaN routing.

Domain Explanation of Key Risk Associations:
  1. schedule_slippage_months_t: Direct historical slippage as of t is the primary baseline indicator of future delay.
  2. physical_progress_t & remaining_physical_progress_t: Low absolute completion combined with advanced project age.
  3. months_to_original_doc_t: Proximity to original target completion date increases delay probability exponentially if progress is lagging.
  4. stagnant_3m_t & longest_stagnation_to_date_t: Multi-month construction stagnation is a severe predictor of persistent distress.
  5. expenditure_ratio_pct_t & cost_escalation_pct_t: High capital expenditure relative to physical progress indicates financial/operational bottleneck.
  * Note: Feature importance reflects statistical associations with future risk; does NOT assert direct causal mechanisms.

11. LIMITATIONS & KNOWN CONSTRAINTS
-----------------------------------
1. Macro Reporting Structure: July-Nov 2025 focused cohort created higher label unavailability in train set.
2. Exogenous Shocks: Weather, environmental clearance, and land litigation not explicitly recorded in quantitative tables.
3. Administrative Lags: Formal revised DOC updates sometimes lag physical ground reality by 1-2 months.
